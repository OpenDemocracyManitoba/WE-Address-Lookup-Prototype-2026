# Use concept-specific authoritative sources

Official election sources can disagree or omit information, as demonstrated by the City address data including Seine River School Division Ward 1 while the City's 2026 school-division boundary page omits it. Each kind of election information therefore has a designated authoritative source: City address data for Electoral Assignments, Senior Election Official publications for Candidate Status, and official City or school-division records for Contest structure and Number to Elect. The ingestion system records provenance for imported information and sends unresolved source conflicts for human review instead of guessing or silently discarding records.
