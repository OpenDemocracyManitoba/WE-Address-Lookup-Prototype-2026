# About

WinnipegElection.ca is a voter resource built by Open Democracy Manitoba (ODM). ODM is a non-profit organization that builds digital tools for democratic engagement. 

<a href="https://opendemocracymanitoba.ca">Learn more about Open Democracy Manitoba</a>.

## Not Affiliated with the City of Winnipeg

WinnipegElection.ca has no affiliation with the City of Winnipeg. The City Clerk’s Department, which is the official Election authority for city council and school trustee elections, runs <a href="https://www.winnipeg.ca/city-governance/wards-elections/2026-election">the official Winnipeg City 2026 Election site</a>.

<h2 id="news-coverage">News Coverage</h2>

Contest pages and address lookup results link to race and candidate profiles from two newsrooms: [CBC Manitoba](https://www.cbc.ca/news/canada/manitoba) and [the Winnipeg Free Press](https://www.winnipegfreepress.com). We limited it to these two so every race is checked against the same sources. Other outlets also covered the election, and leaving them out doesn’t reflect on their reporting.

To find the articles, we went through each outlet’s 2026 election coverage published up to October 26 and matched each piece to the City of Winnipeg’s official list of nominated candidates. We include only pieces that profile a race or a candidate, such as ward profiles, candidate interviews and platform guides, and leave out day-to-day campaign news.

School trustee races have no news links because so few were covered that listing them would single out individual candidates.

Links go to the publishers’ own sites, and some Free Press stories need a subscription.

## How Is This Website Funded?

Ongoing site development and maintenance is performed on a volunteer basis by the board members of [Open Democracy Manitoba](https://opendemocracymanitoba.ca).

## How Do You Decide What Candidate Info to List?

All candidate information, including contact details, social media, and disclosure documents, is sourced from [the City of Winnipeg's official election candidates open dataset](https://data.winnipeg.ca/d/9gi9-dauz). Candidate-related news articles are sourced from CBC Manitoba and the Winnipeg Free Press. See [above](#news-coverage) for news coverage details.
